# Trekipelago 🌍 

**A real-world movement and exploration randomizer for Archipelago MultiWorld.**

Have you ever wanted to turn your daily walk, run, or bike ride into an Archipelago experience? **Trekipelago** is a mobile client that connects your real-world physical activity to the Archipelago network. You generate checks for other players by walking and exploring your neighborhood, while their progress unlocks upgrades for your tracking capabilities.

## 🚀 Concept & Gameplay

The core loop of the game revolves around real-world movement and location-based interactions. 

### 📍 How to send checks (Locations)
Instead of defeating bosses or finding chests in a virtual world, you earn checks by being active:
* **Distance Milestones:** Walking or traveling a specific distance.
* **Light Orbs:** Finding and collecting spawned "Light Orbs" on your real-world map.
* **Treasure Boxes:** Reaching specific Points of Interest (POIs) generated via OpenStreetMap data (e.g., viewpoints, resting areas, and other local landmarks).
* **Logic Tasks:** Solving specific in-game puzzles or tasks during your journey.

### 🎁 What you receive (Items)
When other players find your items in their games, you receive upgrades that make your real-world exploration easier and more efficient:
* **Background Tracking:** Unlocks the ability to count distance while the phone screen is off (a crucial early-game progression item!).
* **Auto-Collect:** Automatically gathers Light Orbs within your radius.
* **Radius Upgrades:** Expands your radar vision and orb collection range.
* **Speed Limit Upgrades:** Increases the maximum allowed speed, allowing you to track progress while jogging or cycling.
* **Distance Multipliers:** Boosts the amount of distance logged per physical step.

### ☠️ Penalties (Traps)
Archipelago wouldn't be complete without traps sent by your friends!
* **Progress Halved:** Divides your collected distance or orbs for a set amount of time or distance.
* **Map Blackout:** Temporarily turns off your map UI, forcing you to walk blind.
* **Logic Locks:** Forces you to solve a logic puzzle before the app resumes counting your distance.
